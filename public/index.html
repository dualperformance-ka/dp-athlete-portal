Priority Order
1
Hit protein every day. Non-negotiable — protects muscle and keeps you full.
2
Stay within your calorie target. Ceiling on rest days, floor on hard training days.
3
Hit your fibre target. 31–40g daily from vegetables, fruit, oats and legumes.
4
Carbs and fat fill around protein. Use carbs around training, fat fills the rest.

Key Principles
Never train fasted during speed or long runs. Saturday night is your most important meal — eat 80–100g carbs (pasta, rice, noodles) to top off glycogen for Sunday's long run. Spread protein across 4–5 meals, aiming for 35–55g per main meal.
